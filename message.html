Two paths divide the hush of night:

One strange and dark, one calm and bright.

To the left, the shadows curl and creep,

Where tangled roots guard secrets deep.

A neon thread winds out of sight,

And draws you farther from the light.

Each turn reveals a dream unknown;

Each whispered clue is yours alone.


To the right, a lantern burns,

And toward the waking world it turns.

No riddles wait, no shadows cling;

Just morning light on everything.

Step through; let restless visions cease.

Beyond, the waking world brings peace.


One road keeps secrets just ahead;

One leads you from the dreams you dread.

Your footsteps pause. The choice is wide:

Will you go home—or step inside?
